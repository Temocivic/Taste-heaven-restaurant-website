Taste Haven began with a simple idea: make a place where thoughtful cooking and generous hospitality belong at the same table. Our kitchen follows the seasons and works with ingredients we love.

Chef Layla Mansour leads a team that honours classic technique while giving every plate a fresh point of view. From the first coffee of the morning to a long dinner with friends, we want you to feel at home.
